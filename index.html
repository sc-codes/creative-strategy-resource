Objective hierarchy — what does “objective” mean?
Business objective

The commercial outcome the company needs.

Acne example: grow profitable revenue for the new acne line.

→
Marketing / campaign objective

What marketing must cause or support.

Example: acquire new customers or build awareness for barrier-first treatment.

→
Creative objective / KPI

What this asset must accomplish in the communication chain.

Example: earn the stop, explain the mechanism, increase qualified clicks, or overcome an objection.

Rule: do not optimize a creative metric that is disconnected from the larger commercial objective.

Awareness vs sophistication vs funnel position
Awareness

What this person knows.

Unaware → Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware → Product-Aware → Most-Aware.

Market sophistication

What the market has already heard.

A mature acne market may already be saturated with ingredient, dermatologist and “clear skin fast” claims.

TOF / MOF / BOF

Where someone sits in your commercial funnel or relationship.

Someone can be TOF yet highly Product-Aware because she researched the brand elsewhere.

Decision authority — what governs what?

There is no single universal authority hierarchy. Different sources govern different questions.

Product, legal + factual truth

Governs what you can truthfully claim. Performance never overrides factual accuracy.

Customer evidence

Governs what pains, desires, language, beliefs and situations appear relevant to real customers.

Business + brand strategy

Governs positioning, priorities, economics and which opportunities are strategically worth pursuing.

Official platform guidance

Governs specs, placement constraints and platform metric definitions.

Practitioner frameworks

Supply useful models, heuristics and hypotheses. They are tools, not universal laws.

Your measured results

Govern account-specific optimization: what deserves expansion, iteration or retirement.

Platform execution terms
Placement behavior

How people consume content in that placement: scrolling, tapping, sound behavior, skip behavior and surrounding UI.

Creative specs

Technical rules: aspect ratio, dimensions, duration, file type, audio support and related requirements.

Safe zones

Areas where faces, text, products and CTAs are less likely to be covered by interface elements.

Native execution

Creative that feels appropriate to the platform’s normal visual and behavioral language without confusing “native” with careless.
